# US-07.2 — Annual Credit and Proration
**Epic:** E-07 | **Feature:** F-07 | **FRs:** FR-33 to FR-35

## User Story
As the system, I want to credit annual leave balances on January 1st and prorate for employees who join mid-year so that balances are always accurate.

## Acceptance Criteria
- [ ] AC-47: NewYearCreditJob runs on 31 Dec 18:30 UTC (= 01 Jan 00:00 IST); credits all active employees
- [ ] AC-48: ProrateForNewJoiner: credit = (remaining_months_in_year / 12) × annual_entitlement, rounded to 0.5
- [ ] AC-49: CreditAnnual creates new leave_balance row for new year; does not overwrite existing
